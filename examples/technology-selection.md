# Choose a technology

Question: Which of the named candidate libraries meets the stated integration
requirements? Fill in the candidates and requirements before researching.

Scope: Official documentation, compatibility requirements and licence sources.
Separate documented capabilities from inference. Do not install or acquire
anything. Do not invent a recommendation if the requirements are incomplete.

Answer: Claims per candidate, citations, falsifiers, limitations and unanswered
questions. Include what evidence would change the recommendation.
